Campus Connect

Bridging the Gap Between Social Connection and Academic Success

Presented by [Jesse Tyohom]
November 2025

(Visual: Campus Connect Logo with a collage of students studying and using the app)

The Problem

Student Isolation & Fragmented Tools

  • Disconnected Experience: Juggling Zoom, Discord, Canvas, WhatsApp.
  • Lack of Engagement: "Zoom fatigue" and isolation in remote/hybrid learning.
  • Academic Friction: Hard to find study partners or quick help.
  • No Incentive: Traditional tools are boring and unrewarding.
(Visual: A chaotic web of icons vs. a lonely student)

The Solution

Campus Connect

An all-in-one platform designed to make learning social, interactive, and rewarding.

  • Unified Hub: Chat, video, and study tools in one place.
  • Purpose-Built: Designed for university life, not just work.
  • AI-Enhanced: Intelligent tools to unblock learning.
  • Gamified: Making studying feel like progress.
(Visual: Dashboard showing "Glassmorphism" UI)

Core Pillars

  1. Connect (Squads & Chat): Find your tribe based on major/interests.
  2. Collaborate (Live Spaces): Drop-in audio/video rooms.
  3. Learn (Lumina): AI-powered visual explanations.
  4. Grow (Leveling System): Earn XP, unlock badges, climb leaderboards.
(Visual: Four icons representing the pillars)

Squads & Messaging

More Than Just Chat

  • Persistent Groups: For classes, clubs, or dorms.
  • Real-Time: Built on Supabase for instant sync.
  • Split-View Interface: Chat without context switching.
  • Smart Notifications: Signal over noise.
(Visual: Split-View Messages Page)

Live Spaces

Immersive Collaboration

  • Powered by Daily.co: High-quality video/audio.
  • Specialized Modes:
    • Study Session (Focus timers, lo-fi)
    • Lecture (Host controls)
    • Social Hangout (Games)
  • Dark Mode UI: Reduced eye strain.
(Visual: Live Space interface with video grid)

Lumina AI

"Turn 'I don't get it' into 'That's amazing!'"

  • Text-to-Animation: "Show me how a derivative works."
  • Powered by Manim & Gemini: Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash scripts 3Blue1Brown's engine.
  • Instant Understanding: Visualizes abstract concepts.
(Visual: Lumina interface with math animation)

Gamification

Level Up Your Learning

  • XP System: Earn points for engagement.
  • Progression: Novice → Master.
  • Badges: "Squad Creator", "Chat Master".
  • Leaderboards: Friendly competition.
(Visual: User profile with Level 5 badge)

Technical Architecture

Modern, Scalable, Fast

  • Frontend: Next.js 15 + Tailwind + Shadcn UI
  • Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime)
  • Video: Daily.co API (WebRTC)
  • AI: Gemini 2.5 Flash + Python (Manim)
  • Deployment: Vercel / Docker
(Visual: System architecture diagram)

The Future Roadmap

  • Mobile App: Native iOS/Android.
  • Cross-Campus Federation: Connect universities.
  • AI Tutor: Voice conversation in Live Spaces.
  • Marketplace: Share notes/tutoring.

Join us in redefining the campus experience.
