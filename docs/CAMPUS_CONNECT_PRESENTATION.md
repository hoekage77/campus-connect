## Slide 2: The Problem

**Student Isolation & Fragmented Tools**

*   **Disconnected Experience:** Students juggle multiple apps—Zoom for calls, Discord for chat, Canvas for grades, WhatsApp for groups.
*   **Lack of Engagement:** Remote and hybrid learning can feel isolating. "Zoom fatigue" is real.
*   **Academic Friction:** Finding a study partner or a quick explanation for a complex concept is harder than it should be.
*   **No Incentive:** Traditional study tools are boring and don't reward consistent effort.

*(Visual: A chaotic web of icons (Zoom, Slack, Email) vs. a lonely student)*

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

**Campus Connect**

An all-in-one platform designed to make learning social, interactive, and rewarding.

*   **Unified Hub:** Chat, video, and study tools in one place.
*   **Purpose-Built:** Designed specifically for university life—not just "work" or "gaming".
*   **AI-Enhanced:** Intelligent tools to unblock learning instantly.
*   **Gamified:** Making studying feel like progress, not a chore.

*(Visual: Screenshot of the Campus Connect Dashboard showing the "Glassmorphism" UI)*

## Slide 4: Core Pillars

1.  **Connect (Squads & Chat):** Find your tribe based on major, year, or interests.
2.  **Collaborate (Live Spaces):** Drop-in audio/video rooms for study sessions, office hours, or hangouts.
3.  **Learn (Lumina):** AI-powered visual explanations for complex math and physics concepts.
4.  **Grow (Leveling System):** Earn XP, unlock badges, and climb the leaderboard.

*(Visual: Four icons representing the pillars, arranged in a grid)*

## Slide 5: Feature Deep Dive - Squads & Messaging

**More Than Just Chat**

*   **Persistent Groups:** Create "Squads" for classes, clubs, or dorms.
*   **Real-Time Messaging:** Built on Supabase for instant communication.
*   **Split-View Interface:** Chat while browsing other content—no context switching.
*   **Smart Notifications:** Stay updated on what matters without the noise.

*(Visual: The new Split-View Messages Page with a conversation open)*

---

## Slide 6: Feature Deep Dive - Live Spaces

**Immersive Collaboration**

*   **Powered by Daily.co:** High-quality, low-latency video and audio.
*   **Specialized Modes:**
    *   *Study Session:* Focus timers and lo-fi music.
    *   *Lecture:* Host controls and hand-raising.
    *   *Social Hangout:* Games and casual vibes.
*   **Dark Mode UI:** "Immersive" design to reduce eye strain during late-night sessions.

*(Visual: The "Live Space" interface with video grid and floating controls)*

## Slide 7: Feature Deep Dive - Lumina AI

**"Turn 'I don't get it' into 'That's amazing!'"**

*   **Text-to-Animation:** Ask a question like "Show me how a derivative works," and get a video explanation.
*   **Powered by Manim & Gemini:** Uses 3Blue1Brown's animation engine scripted by Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash.
*   **Instant Understanding:** Visualizes abstract concepts in seconds.

*(Visual: A screenshot of the Lumina interface with a generated math animation)*

## Slide 8: Gamification & Engagement

**Level Up Your Learning**

*   **XP System:** Earn points for attending lectures, sending messages, and helping others.
*   **Progression:** Advance from "Novice" to "Master".
*   **Badges:** Unlock achievements like "Squad Creator," "Event Enthusiast," and "Chat Master."
*   **Leaderboards:** Friendly competition to keep motivation high.

## Slide 9: Technical Architecture

**Modern, Scalable, Fast**

*   **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Shadcn UI.
*   **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime Subscriptions).
*   **Video Infrastructure:** Daily.co API for scalable WebRTC.
*   **AI Engine:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash + Python (Manim) running in Daytona/Containers.
*   **Deployment:** Vercel / Docker.
