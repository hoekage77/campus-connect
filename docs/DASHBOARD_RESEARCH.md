# Dashboard Experience Research & Strategy

**Date:** November 15, 2025  
**Purpose:** Research popular platform dashboards to create a unique, curated Campus Connect experience

---

## Platform Analysis

### 1. **LinkedIn** - Professional Network Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Feed**: Curated posts from connections + recommended content
- **Recent Activity**: Who viewed your profile, post analytics
- **Upcoming Events**: Webinars, networking events
- **Job Recommendations**: Based on profile/interests
- **Skills to Learn**: Personalized course suggestions
- **People You May Know**: Network expansion

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ Action-oriented cards ("Complete your profile")
- ✅ Social proof (engagement metrics, mutual connections)
- ✅ Future-focused (upcoming events, opportunities)
- ✅ Personalized recommendations
- ❌ Can feel overwhelming with too many suggestions

---

### 2. **Discord** - Community Platform Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Active Now**: Friends/server members currently online
- **Direct Messages**: Recent conversations
- **Server List**: Joined communities with unread indicators
- **Friend Activity**: What friends are doing (games, Spotify)
- **Nitro Promotions**: Platform features

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ Real-time presence ("who's online now")
- ✅ Unread/notification-driven navigation
- ✅ Community-first (servers before DMs)
- ✅ Friend activity creates FOMO/engagement
- ✅ Minimalist, fast, focused on action

---

### 3. **Notion** - Productivity Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Recently Viewed**: Quick access to recent work
- **Favorites**: Pinned pages
- **Templates**: Getting started inspiration
- **Shared with Me**: Collaboration items
- **Custom Dashboards**: User creates their own view

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ Quick access over notifications
- ✅ User customization (create your own dashboard)
- ✅ Work-in-progress visibility
- ✅ Templates for inspiration
- ✅ Clean, distraction-free

---

### 4. **GitHub** - Developer Platform Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Recent Activity**: Your commits, PRs, issues
- **Repositories**: Active projects
- **Contribution Graph**: Visual progress over time
- **Explore**: Trending repos, topics of interest
- **Following**: Activity from people you follow

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ Visual progress tracking (contribution heatmap)
- ✅ Your work first, then discovery
- ✅ Activity feed shows what YOU did
- ✅ Trending/discovery for inspiration
- ✅ Achievement-oriented (streaks, contributions)

---

### 5. **Duolingo** - Learning Platform Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Daily Goal Progress**: XP earned today
- **Streak Counter**: Days learning consecutively
- **Lessons Available**: Next steps to take
- **Leaderboard**: Friends/global ranking
- **Achievements**: Badges and milestones
- **Friends Activity**: Social comparison

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ **PROGRESS FIRST** - "Here's what you've accomplished"
- ✅ Gamification (streaks, XP, leaderboards)
- ✅ Clear next action ("Continue lesson")
- ✅ Social comparison drives engagement
- ✅ Celebration of small wins

---

### 6. **Strava** - Fitness Community Dashboard
**What They Show:**
- **Your Recent Activities**: Workouts with stats
- **Friends' Activities**: Social feed of workouts
- **Challenges**: Active competitions
- **Personal Records**: PRs and achievements
- **Training Log**: Weekly summary
- **Local Segments**: Leaderboards for routes

**Key Patterns:**
- ✅ Your activities ARE the content
- ✅ Stats and metrics prominent
- ✅ Social feed of real actions (not posts)
- ✅ Challenges create engagement
- ✅ Competition with self and others

---

## Key Insights Summary

### What Works Best:
1. **Progress > Notifications** - Show what user accomplished, not just updates
2. **Action-Oriented** - Clear next steps, not just consumption
3. **Social Proof** - Who's active, what friends are doing
4. **Visual Progress** - Graphs, streaks, achievements
5. **Personalized** - Based on interests, squads, activity
6. **Quick Access** - Recent/active items first
7. **Celebration** - Highlight wins and milestones
8. **Future-Focused** - Upcoming events, opportunities

### What to Avoid:
- ❌ Generic notification dumps
- ❌ Overwhelming choice
- ❌ Passive consumption (no actions)
- ❌ Irrelevant content
- ❌ Buried important actions

## Proposed Dashboard Structure

### Tab 1: **FOR YOU** (Default) 🎯
**Purpose:** Curated, actionable content personalized to user

#### Sections:
1. **Active Now** (Top Priority)
   - Friends/squadmates currently online
   - Live Spaces happening NOW in your squads
   - "Join Study Sprint in CS101" (clickable, urgent)
   - Creates FOMO, drives immediate engagement

2. **Today's Opportunities**
   - Upcoming events in next 4 hours
   - Active study sessions you can join
   - Squads with recent activity (unread messages)
   - "3 people from CS Major Squad are studying now"

3. **Quick Actions**
   - "Start a Study Sprint" (quick create)
   - "Find Study Partner" (interest matching)
   - "Join Popular Space" (trending rooms)

4. **Your Progress Today**
   - XP earned today vs daily goal
   - Streak status with celebration
   - Messages sent, events attended
   - Visual progress bar

5. **Recommended for You**
   - Squads matching your interests (not yet joined)
   - Students with similar classes/majors
   - Upcoming events based on interests
   - Limited to 3-5 high-quality recommendations

6. **Friend Activity** (Social)
   - Recent achievements by squadmates
   - "Sarah just leveled up to Expert!"
   - "Mike joined a new CS Study Group"
   - Squad milestones

---

### Tab 2: **MY ACTIVITY** 📊
**Purpose:** Personal analytics and history

#### Sections:
1. **This Week's Stats**
   - Study hours (from Spaces attendance)
   - Squads active in
   - Messages sent
   - Events attended
   - Contribution graph (GitHub-style heatmap)

2. **Your Content**
   - Messages you posted (in squads)
   - Spaces you hosted
   - Events you created
   - Squads you founded

3. **Achievements & Milestones**
   - Recent badges earned
   - Level progress
   - Streak milestones
   - Upcoming goals

4. **Activity History**
   - Timeline of your actions
   - Squad joins, leaves
   - Event participation

---

### Tab 3: **SQUADS** 👥
**Purpose:** Your communities at a glance

#### Layout:
1. **Active Squads** (Most recent activity first)
   - Unread message count
   - Last activity timestamp
   - "Live now" indicator for Spaces
   - Quick join Space button

2. **Pinned Squads**
   - User-defined favorites at top

3. **Discover**
   - "Squads your friends joined"
   - "Popular in your major"
   - "Matching your interests"

---

### Tab 4: **CALENDAR** 📅
**Purpose:** Time-based view of opportunities

#### Views:
1. **Today**
   - Hourly timeline
   - Scheduled study sessions
   - Office hours
   - Social events

2. **This Week**
   - Grid view of upcoming events
   - Your scheduled Spaces
   - Deadlines (if we add that feature)

3. **Create Event**
   - Quick access to schedule

## Content Curation Rules

### What to Show:
✅ **Time-Sensitive Actions** - "Happening now" or "Soon"
✅ **Personalized** - Based on squads, interests, friends
✅ **Social Context** - "3 of your friends are here"
✅ **Progress** - What you accomplished
✅ **Opportunities** - Things you can DO right now
✅ **Celebration** - Achievements, milestones, wins
✅ **Discovery** - Limited, high-quality recommendations

### What NOT to Show:
❌ Generic notifications dump
❌ Old activity (>24 hours unless significant)
❌ Irrelevant squad updates (squads you're not in)
❌ System messages ("Database updated")
❌ Endless scroll of noise

### Ranking Algorithm:
1. **Live/Urgent** (Spaces active NOW, events starting <1hr)
2. **Personal** (Your squads, friends, interests)
3. **Time-Sensitive** (Events today/tomorrow)
4. **Social Proof** ("5 friends here")
5. **Fresh** (Recent activity >old)
6. **Engagement** (Popular squads/events)

## UI Principles for Campus Connect Dashboard

### 1. **Visual Hierarchy**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🔴 URGENT (Live Spaces, Soon)     │  ← Red/Orange, pulsing
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│  🟢 ACTIVE (Happening Today)       │  ← Green, prominent
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│  🔵 DISCOVER (Recommendations)      │  ← Blue, subtle
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ⚪ STATS (Your Progress)           │  ← Muted, informational
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2. **Interaction Patterns**
- **One-Click Actions**: "Join Space", "RSVP", "Message"
- **Hover Previews**: Quick info without navigation
- **Inline Actions**: Do things without leaving dashboard
- **Optimistic UI**: Instant feedback

### 3. **Empty States**
- Never show "Nothing here"
- Always suggest actions: "Start your first study session"
- Show onboarding for new users
- Highlight discovery for inactive users

### 4. **Mobile-First**
- Vertical card stacking
- Swipeable sections
- Bottom action buttons
- Collapsible sections

## Success Metrics

### Engagement Metrics:
- **Daily Active Users (DAU)** - Users checking dashboard daily
- **Click-Through Rate** - Actions taken from dashboard
- **Time to Action** - How fast users join Spaces/events
- **Return Rate** - Users coming back within 24 hours

### Content Metrics:
- **Space Join Rate** - "Active Now" conversions
- **Event RSVP Rate** - Calendar engagement
- **Squad Discovery** - Recommendations accepted
- **Social Actions** - Friend interactions

### Satisfaction Metrics:
- **Session Duration** - Not too long (means scanning, not reading)
- **Bounce Rate** - Users leaving immediately
- **Feature Usage** - Which sections get clicked
- **User Feedback** - Surveys, ratings

   - Live Spaces prominence
